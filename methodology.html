SCIENTIFIC METHOD

Methodology

How evidence is selected, organized and presented across the Curated Scientific Library and Latest Evidence.

CURATED LIBRARY

Reviewed clinical relevance

Publications are selected for a direct relationship between the clinical field and ketogenic therapy, nutritional ketosis, VLCKD/VLEKT, modified Atkins approaches, ketone supplementation or ketone metabolism when clinically relevant.

LATEST EVIDENCE

Recent literature monitoring

A rolling 90-day PubMed view identifies recent, directly relevant literature. Inclusion here does not mean that a paper has been accepted into the Curated Scientific Library.

Evidence selection pathway

1Direct topical relevance
2Study design and population
3Bibliographic verification
4Clinical-area classification

Evidence hierarchy

Priority is generally given to systematic reviews and meta-analyses, consensus statements and guidelines, recent human clinical studies, older clinical evidence, and finally mechanistic or preclinical evidence. The hierarchy is used to organize evidence, not to assign a numerical score to individual papers.

Link policy

DOI and publisher links are preferred as persistent references. PubMed is used for bibliographic verification and discovery. Legal open-access full text is linked when available. When a direct identifier has not yet been verified, an exact-title PubMed lookup may be used instead of an unverified direct link.

Scope and limitations

This resource is an evidence-navigation project, not a clinical guideline. Inclusion does not imply endorsement or establish treatment efficacy. Interpretation depends on study design, population, intervention, comparator and outcome quality. A publication may appear in more than one clinical area when it is directly relevant to each.
