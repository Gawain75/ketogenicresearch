Scientific Director · Ketogenic Research Hub

Marco Medeot leads the scientific direction of the Ketogenic Research Hub, with a focus on ketogenic nutritional therapy, clinical nutrition and metabolic research.

His scientific interests include obesity, body composition, preservation of lean mass, neurological applications, metabolic physiology and the clinical translation of ketogenic strategies.
