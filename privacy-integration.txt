KETOGENIC RESEARCH SCIENTIFIC LIBRARY
Privacy integration notes — v2026-09-22

1) Registration checkbox
Replace wording such as:
  "Acconsento al trattamento dei dati personali"
or:
  "Ho letto l'informativa sulla privacy"

with:
  <label>
    <input type="checkbox" id="privacy_ack" name="privacy_ack" required>
    Dichiaro di aver preso visione dell’<a href="/privacy.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Informativa Privacy</a>.
  </label>

This checkbox documents acknowledgement/availability of the notice.
It is NOT marketing consent.

2) English registration wording
  <label>
    <input type="checkbox" id="privacy_ack" name="privacy_ack" required>
    I acknowledge that I have read the <a href="/privacy-en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Privacy Notice</a>.
  </label>

3) Privacy version
Save the following version together with the profile/registration record:
  2026-09-22

Suggested field:
  privacy_version = "2026-09-22"

If you also keep an acknowledgement timestamp:
  privacy_acknowledged_at = current timestamp

Do not store a field called "privacy_consent" for account creation, because the legal basis
for the account is the requested service, not consent.

4) Future newsletter
Use a SEPARATE, optional and unticked checkbox, for example:
  <label>
    <input type="checkbox" id="newsletter_consent" name="newsletter_consent">
    Desidero ricevere via email aggiornamenti e comunicazioni dalla Ketogenic Research Scientific Library.
  </label>

Store newsletter consent independently from privacy acknowledgement, with its own timestamp
and the wording/version shown when consent was collected.

5) Footer
Add a permanent link:
  <a href="/privacy.html">Privacy</a>

If the interface has a language selector:
  IT -> /privacy.html
  EN -> /privacy-en.html

6) Current technical cookies
The current Worker uses:
  kr_access_token
  kr_refresh_token

They are authentication/session cookies with Secure and SameSite=Lax.
With the current setup and in the absence of non-essential analytics/advertising trackers,
do not add a consent banner merely for these strictly necessary cookies.

7) Important deployment check
Before publishing, verify:
  - the Supabase project region actually selected in the dashboard;
  - that no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, advertising or other non-essential trackers
    are loaded elsewhere in the site;
  - that the Supabase and Cloudflare contractual/DPA settings applicable to the account are retained;
  - that the registration form fields match the categories described in the notice.
