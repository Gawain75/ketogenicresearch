Privacy Notice

Ketogenic Research Scientific Library — version 22 September 2026

This notice describes the processing of personal data relating to users who register for and access the Ketogenic Research Scientific Library, pursuant to Articles 12, 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”), where applicable.

The Scientific Library is a read-only information service. Users are not expected to enter their own health data, patient data, or other special categories of personal data.

1. Data Controller

Marco Medeot
Italy
Email: info@ketogenicresearch.org

No Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed. Should the conditions under Article 37 GDPR become applicable in the future, this notice will be updated.

2. Personal data processed

During registration and use of the Library, the following data may be processed:

  • identification and contact data, such as first name, last name and email address;
  • professional information where requested in the registration form;
  • account data, email verification data and authentication information;
  • technical data required for service security and operation, such as IP address, request date/time, browser/device information and technical logs;
  • cookies or identifiers strictly necessary to maintain an authenticated session.

Users are not asked to provide health, genetic, biometric or other special-category data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Account creation, management and Library access. Data are processed to register the user, verify the email address, authenticate access and provide the requested service. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Service communications. Communications strictly necessary for account management, security, access recovery or material service changes may be sent. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and, for security-related processing, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Security, abuse prevention and infrastructure protection. Technical data and logs may be processed to protect the website, accounts and systems against unauthorized access, fraud, attacks and technical failures. Legal basis: the Controller’s legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Legal obligations and claims. Data may be retained or disclosed where required to comply with the law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(c) and/or (f) GDPR, as applicable.

4. Newsletter and promotional communications

Registration for the Scientific Library does not currently result in automatic subscription to newsletters or promotional communications. If a newsletter service is introduced in the future, consent will be requested separately and will be optional, specific and unticked by default. Refusing or withdrawing such consent will not affect access to the Library.

5. Requirement to provide data

Data marked as required in the registration form are necessary to create and manage the account. Failure to provide them prevents activation or use of the service. Data expressly marked as optional may be omitted without affecting access.

6. Processing methods and security

Data are processed through electronic systems in accordance with the principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, data minimisation and storage limitation. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are adopted to protect data against loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

7. Service providers and recipients

To operate the service, the Controller uses technical providers that may process personal data as processors or sub-processors according to their contractual roles, including:

  • Supabase, for authentication, account management and database services;
  • Cloudflare, for website hosting/serving, network services, security and infrastructure protection.

Data may also be disclosed to public bodies, authorities or professional advisers where required by law or necessary to protect a legal right. Personal data are not sold or disclosed to third parties for their own independent commercial purposes.

8. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers or their sub-processors may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where processing involves an international transfer, it is carried out in accordance with Chapter V GDPR, using, as applicable, adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or other safeguards permitted by applicable law.

9. Retention

Account data are retained for the lifetime of the account. After account deletion, data are deleted or anonymised from active systems without undue delay, except where retention is strictly necessary for legal obligations, security, abuse prevention, dispute management and the normal technical rotation of provider backups. Technical logs are retained only for the period necessary for security and diagnostic purposes, applying proportionality and data-minimisation criteria.

10. Cookies and technical identifiers

The Library uses technical identifiers strictly necessary for authentication and session maintenance, including access- and refresh-session cookies. These tools are used solely to provide the requested service and for security purposes. At present, the Library does not use profiling cookies or tracking technologies for behavioural advertising.

No consent is required for strictly necessary technical identifiers, without prejudice to the obligation to provide appropriate information.

11. Automated decision-making and profiling

Personal data are not used for decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect users. The Scientific Library does not perform commercial profiling of registered users.

12. Data subject rights

Where provided by the GDPR, data subjects may exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Where a future processing activity is based on consent, consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Requests may be sent to info@ketogenicresearch.org.

13. Complaint to a supervisory authority

Data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or, where provided by the GDPR, with the competent supervisory authority in the Member State of habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement.

14. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated following regulatory, technical or organisational changes. The current version is identified by the date shown at the top of the page. Where a change materially affects the processing of registered users’ personal data, appropriate information will be provided.
