01 / ABOUT

A research hub focused on evidence, physiology and translation.

Ketogenic Research Hub develops and supports research on ketogenic nutritional strategies and their applications across health and medicine.

The objective is to investigate clinically relevant questions through a rigorous, evidence-informed approach, integrating metabolic physiology, nutrition science and translational research.

03 / SCIENTIFIC DIRECTION

Marco Medeot

Scientific Director · Ketogenic Research Hub

Marco Medeot is Scientific Director of the Ketogenic Research Hub in Manoppello, Italy, where he coordinates research and development activities in ketogenic nutrition and the clinical validation of nutritional protocols.

His scientific interests include ketogenic nutritional therapy, obesity, body composition, preservation of lean mass, neurological applications and emerging metabolic approaches.
