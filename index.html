01 / ABOUT

A research hub focused on evidence, physiology and translation.

Ketogenic Research Hub develops and supports clinical and translational research investigating ketogenic nutritional strategies and their applications across health and medicine.

Our work connects metabolic physiology, clinical research and the development of evidence-informed protocols, with a focus on questions where nutritional ketosis may have a relevant biological or therapeutic role.

03 / SCIENTIFIC DIRECTION

Marco Medeot

Scientific Director · Ketogenic Research Hub

Marco Medeot is Scientific Director of the Ketogenic Research Hub in Manoppello, Italy, where he coordinates research and development activities in ketogenic nutrition and the clinical validation of nutritional protocols.

His work includes clinical and translational research, with interests in ketogenic nutritional therapy, obesity and body composition, preservation of lean mass, neurological applications and emerging metabolic approaches.

He is also founder of KetoEducation Academy and has taught in professional and academic settings on ketogenic therapies, longevity and clinical nutrition.
